## Frequently Asked Questions

[What are BrazeAI™ Agents?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): BrazeAI™ Agents are autonomous AI assistants built into the Braze platform that help marketers work faster and smarter. They are highly configurable and can be deployed across the Braze platform, using your customers’ data and real-time context to automate tasks, personalize campaigns at scale, and help improve results across channels like email, push, in-app, and more.

[What types of agents does Braze offer?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): Braze offers three complementary types of agents. Marketers can easily build agents with Operator for common marketing tasks, like content generation and feedback analysis. Custom agents are built in the BrazeAI Agent Console™, and let you design task-specific, LLM-powered assistants that plug in directly in your Braze workflows. AI decisioning agents, available through BrazeAI Decisioning Studio™, make decisions at the individual customer level to optimize any business KPI. These agents can personalize channel, offer, message, creative, timing, frequency and more. They autonomously experiment and continuously learn from every customer interaction.

[How does the BrazeAI Agent Console™ work?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): Agent Console is an intuitive dashboard where marketers can create and manage custom agents without coding. You define the task, choose an AI model, and provide instructions (in the form of a prompt) for the agent. Agents are then invoked in either Catalogs or Canvases, to enrich data or personalize experiences, respectively. Currently, we are seeing customers use BrazeAI™ Agents to score leads, translate and localize content, and adapt communications based on customer behavior.

[How are custom agents different from conventional marketing automation?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): While traditional marketing automation runs on fixed rules and workflows defined in advance, custom agents bring AI models directly into Braze to generate outputs and route customers dynamically through Canvases. Instead of just following if/then logic, agents can enrich data, personalize content, and adapt to customer behavior in real time, giving marketers more flexibility and intelligence than conventional automation alone.

[What are common use cases for BrazeAI™ Agents?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): Brands can use BrazeAI™ Agents to tackle a range of marketing challenges, from driving conversions with personalized recommendations to reducing churn through timely offers, while also improving efficiency with tasks like scaling localization, enriching customer profiles, and automating campaign QA checks. Because agents can be shaped to fit each brand’s needs, they make it possible to scale everyday tasks and complex personalization in ways never before possible with Braze.

[Which AI models power BrazeAI™ Agents?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): BrazeAI™ Agents support leading large language models from providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, Microsoft Foundry, Databricks, and Google Gemini, giving marketers flexibility to choose their preferred LLM. AI decisioning agents rely on reinforcement learning, a type of AI best-fit for marketing personalization that optimizes customer and business value. With the Braze Model Context Protocol (MCP), you can also securely connect external AI tools to your Braze data for more advanced use cases.

[What is the difference between BrazeAI Decisioning Studio™ Go and BrazeAI Decisioning Studio™ Pro?](https://www.braze.com/product/ai-agents): BrazeAI Decisioning Studio™ Pro puts full-strength 1:1 decisioning behind your highest-stakes journeys. BrazeAI Decisioning Studio™ Go brings 1:1 decisioning to every journey, self-serve.

[What is a Monthly Active User? What is a Profile? Why are they important?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): A Monthly Active User (MAU) is any unique person who records a session on your Braze-connected app or website during a calendar month. You decide who counts: you can track every visitor if you value anonymous traffic, or limit tracking to only logged-in users. A Profile is the digital record of that person. It stores the user traits and behaviors that allow you to send truly personalized messages. Because your total user base is typically larger than your active monthly audience, every MAU in your plan includes a large allotment of User Profiles. This allows you to store data and re-engage customers with the right message, even if they haven't visited your app or website in a while.

[What products and channels does Action Credits give me access to?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Braze Action Credits unlock the flexible use of any of the following features throughout your contract: Email, SMS/MMS, RCS, WhatsApp, LINE, KakaoTalk, Content Cards, Banners, Message Archiving, Audience Sync, Webhooks, BrazeAI Agent Console™, and BrazeAI Decisioning Studio™ Go. New and exciting features may be added to Action Credits in the future, giving you instant access to use them upon release.

[How do Action Credits work?](https://www.braze.com/pricing): Think of Action Credits as a single, flexible currency for your Braze account. Instead of purchasing fixed quantities for individual products, you buy one pool of credits as part of your contract. You can then use those credits across different channels and features based on your engagement strategy. Each action consumes a different number of credits—for example, sending an SMS may cost 1 credit, while a WhatsApp message may cost 5. Because all usage draws from the same pool, you can easily shift spend (e.g., from Email to Agent Console) without needing to contract a new product. Credits give you the flexibility to adapt your strategy as your customer needs change and your business strategy evolves.

[What is an example of real-time marketing?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/real-time-marketing): A price-drop alert is a common example. [What data does real-time marketing require?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/real-time-marketing): Real-time marketing needs customer events that stream in as they happen and segments that refresh as that data changes. [What is lead nurturing?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/lead-nurturing): Lead nurturing is the practice of building a relationship with someone who has shown interest but hasn't yet purchased, by sending a planned sequence of relevant messages that respond to their behavior until they buy or disengage.

B2B nurture often supports a considered purchase made by several people, with lead scoring and a handoff to a sales rep. [How long should a nurture journey be?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/lead-nurturing): A nurture journey should last as long as it takes each person to either buy or show they’re not ready. [What is the difference between lead nurturing and a promotional campaign?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/lead-nurturing): A promotional campaign is built around an offer, such as a seasonal sale, and is usually scheduled by the business for a chosen segment. Lead nurturing is built around where each person is in their journey. Each person enters when they take an action, receives messages based on what they do, and leaves when they reach a defined outcome.

[When does lead nurturing end?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/lead-nurturing): Lead nurturing ends at the first purchase. That purchase should act as a defined trigger, removing the person from every nurture journey and moving them into retention programs at the same moment. [What is targeted email marketing?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/targeted-email-marketing): Targeted email marketing is the practice of deciding who receives an email based on what you know about them. [What is the difference between targeted email marketing and email personalization?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/targeted-email-marketing): Targeting decides who receives an email, and personalization decides what that email contains. [How small should an email segment be?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/targeted-email-marketing): Small enough that everyone in it has a clear reason to get the message, and large enough to measure. [Is buying an email list a form of targeting?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/targeted-email-marketing): No. A bought list is made up of people who never agreed to hear from you, so you have no consent to email them and no behavior to base targeting on. [What is funnel optimization?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/funnel-optimization): Funnel optimization is the practice of measuring how many people move from one stage of a funnel to the next, identifying the stage with the largest drop-off relative to what's achievable, and making changes at that stage to fix the issues and lift conversions.

[What is the difference between funnel optimization and conversion rate optimization?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/funnel-optimization): Conversion rate optimization focuses on improving a specific page or flow, such as a sign-up form or checkout, so more people complete it. Funnel optimization is broader. It looks across every stage of the funnel, including what happens between visits, to find where the most people are lost. The two are complementary and work best together.

[Which funnel stage should you optimize first?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/funnel-optimization): Start with the stage where the most people are lost relative to what’s achievable, based on your own history, segments, or past results. Then diagnose the cause at that stage before choosing a fix. The same drop-off rate can have different causes at different stages, and the wrong fix can make things worse.

[How do you know a funnel change worked?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/funnel-optimization): Compare the people who received the change with a holdout group who didn’t. Measure both on a fixed cohort, use an attribution window long enough to capture conversions that happen on a later visit, and state which denominator every rate uses. The difference between the two groups is the lift the change created.

[What is an engagement funnel?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/funnel-optimization): An engagement funnel tracks how people interact with a brand across the whole relationship, from awareness through conversion and on into retention and loyalty. [How is customer journey optimization different from customer journey automation?](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/customer-journey-optimization): Customer journey automation is how you build and run a journey; customer journey optimization improves it after it’s live.
