# Kelley Blue Book

> Kelley Blue Book (KBB.com) is a US automotive valuation and research site, founded in 1926 and part of Cox Automotive. It publishes the Kelley Blue Book Value — the reference price used across the US car market — plus expert and consumer reviews, specifications, pricing, and ownership costs for essentially every car, truck, SUV, and van sold in the United States since the 1990s.

How to use this site:

- **Values are the core product, and they are conditional.** "Fair Purchase Price" is what buyers are actually paying in a given area; "Fair Market Range" brackets it; "Trade-In Value" and "Private Party Value" apply when selling. These differ from MSRP and from dealer invoice. All of them depend on ZIP code, mileage, and condition, so a KBB value quoted without those inputs is incomplete and should not be presented as a single national number.
- **Vehicle URLs are constructible**, which matters because this index lists makes but not all 2,561 models. The pattern is:

      /<make>/                          e.g. /toyota/            — full lineup for a make
      /<make>/<model>/                  e.g. /toyota/camry/      — current model overview
      /<make>/<model>/<year>/           e.g. /toyota/camry/2024/ — that model year
      /<make>/<model>/<year>/<trim>/    e.g. /honda/civic/2024/ex-sedan-4d/
      /<make>/<model>/<year>/specs/     specifications and options by trim
      /<make>/<model>/consumer-reviews/ owner reviews
      /<make>/<model>/deals-incentives/ current lease, finance, and rebate offers

  Slugs are lowercase with hyphens (`mercedes-benz`, `land-rover`, `f150-supercrew-cab`). The make pages listed under "Vehicle Research by Make" each show that make's full model lineup, and are the reliable place to read exact model slugs rather than guessing them.
- **Coverage is historical.** Discontinued makes (Oldsmobile, Pontiac, Saturn, Scion, Plymouth, Mercury, Geo, Daewoo, HUMMER) are retained so used vehicles can still be valued. A make appearing here does not mean it currently sells new cars.
- **Editorial content lives under a few flat prefixes:** `/car-news/<slug>/` (news), `/car-advice/<slug>/` (how-to), `/what-is/<slug>/` (term definitions), `/comparison/<slug>/` (head-to-head), `/awards/<slug>/`, `/best-cars/<slug>/` (themed rankings), `/cpo/<slug>/` (certified pre-owned programs by brand).
- Pages are served as HTML only; markdown (`.md`) versions are not currently available.
- There are roughly 104,600 indexable pages. This file lists entry points, not the archive; see sitemap index under Optional for the exhaustive list.
