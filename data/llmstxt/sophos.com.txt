# Sophos: Cyber Resilience for the Agentic AI Era

> Sophos is a cybersecurity company that provides security products, managed security services, threat intelligence, incident response and cyber defense capabilities. Sophos Fusion, the evolution of Sophos Central, is how customers and partners experience the Sophos AI-Native Cybersecurity Defense System, which connects Sophos and 500+ third-party control points across endpoint, network, email, identity and cloud. Sophos offerings include Sophos Endpoint, Sophos MDR, Sophos XDR, Sophos Firewall, Sophos Email, Sophos ITDR, Sophos NDR, Sophos Next-Gen SIEM, Sophos ZTNA, Sophos Workspace Protection and Cloud workload protection.

> Named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Endpoint Protection Platforms for the 17th consecutive time.

> Named a 2026 Gartner® Peer Insights™ Customers' Choice for Endpoint Protection Platforms, Managed Detection and Response, and Email Security.

> Rated the #1 overall solution in EPP, MDR, XDR and Firewall in the G2 Fall 2026 Reports.

> Named a Leader in the 2026 IDC MarketScape for Worldwide Managed Detection and Response (MDR) Service for Midmarket and in the 2026 KuppingerCole Leadership Compass for Managed Detection and Response.

> Sophos reports that more than 625,000 organizations are defended by Sophos Fusion (sophos.com, October 2026). Sophos Ltd is incorporated in England and Wales, and Joe Levy is Chief Executive Officer.
