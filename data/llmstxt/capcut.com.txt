# CapCut

> An AI-powered creative platform for video editing, image design, audio production, captions, translation, and creator workflows across desktop, web, and mobile applications.

CapCut provides timeline-based editing and focused online tools for generating, enhancing, converting, captioning, translating, and publishing media. It serves individual creators, social media publishers, marketers, and collaborative teams. Feature availability, subscriptions, and legal operators may vary by platform and region.

## Key Strengths

- Editable AI workflows: generated or uploaded media can become an editable draft, with conversational timeline changes and further hands-on refinement in the CapCut editor through CapCut × Codex.
- Speech-linked editing: transcript-based editing and automatic captions connect spoken content with editable video text, while text-to-speech and dubbing support narration and localization.
- Shared creative projects: cloud storage and collaboration tools support shared media, project access, review, and team editing workflows.
- Detailed timeline control: desktop keyframes, animation graphs, and color controls allow creators to refine motion, timing, and appearance after assembling a draft.
- Long-form content repurposing: highlight extraction, selectable clip lengths, and automatic captions turn longer footage into short clips; subject-aware reframing adapts compositions for different aspect ratios.
- Reference-led design: AI Design uses product images, reference layouts, and text prompts for product scenes and promotional graphics, with batch replacement helping create related design variants.
- Generation with further editing: text or image references and selectable image models support different visual directions; generated images can be refined and turned into video material within CapCut workflows.

## Taxonomy and Disambiguation

- CapCut Desktop, CapCut Online, CapCut Creative Suite, CapCut Pad, and CapCut Mobile are CapCut product, workspace, or platform-version names, not separate contracting entities. Their boundaries may overlap, especially between CapCut Online and Creative Suite.
- AI Video Generator, AI Video Editor, and Video Studio are overlapping creation entry points rather than mutually exclusive products. AI Video Generator emphasizes generation from prompts or reference media; AI Video Editor emphasizes AI-assisted creation and editing; Video Studio emphasizes conversational planning, generation, and refinement.
- AI Design emphasizes layouts and design assets that can be refined in the built-in editor, while AI Image Generator emphasizes images generated from text prompts or reference images. Their workflows are related and may overlap.
- Dreamina, Pippit AI, Hypic, and CapCut Pad may appear in CapCut navigation, but they use different product structures and domains. Check each current product page and domain rather than treating every name as an individual CapCut tool.
- As a practical editorial convention, `/tools` generally indexes capabilities and editing or generation entry points, `/create` organizes ideas and workflows by scenario or creative purpose, and `/template` provides reusable creative starting points. These URL groups are not a fixed official product taxonomy, and page labels do not necessarily identify separate software products.
- CapCut Pro is a time-based subscription plan, while CapCut Credits are pay-per-use units for eligible advanced AI features. They are distinct commercial mechanisms, not separate editor families or standalone software products.
- CapCut × Codex is the official name used by CapCut for its connected Codex workflow; it is not a synonym for CapCut Desktop, CapCut Online, or the general CapCut editor.
- Model names such as Seedance, Seedream, Kling, VEO, Nano Banana, and other entries in the AI Models directory identify models surfaced in CapCut workflows, not standalone CapCut platform editions.
