# Squarespace

> Squarespace is an all-in-one website-building and ecommerce platform. This
> file indexes resources useful for answering questions about Squarespace
> plans, pricing, and features.

Squarespace offers four subscription tiers (Basic, Core, Plus, Advanced),
each available in monthly or annual billing across the supported currencies
listed in pricing.txt.

## Pricing

* [Full pricing details (all plans, all currencies)](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing.txt): Inlined breakdown of every plan tier across all supported currencies and billing periods.
* [Pricing page](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing): Human-readable pricing comparison. Note: requires JavaScript to render — use pricing.txt above for machine-readable pricing data.

## Plans

* [Squarespace Basic](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing.txt#squarespace-basic): Entry-level Squarespace website plan. Includes a custom domain (free for one year on annual billing), unlimited bandwidth, SSL security, mobile-optimized templates, SEO tools, and basic analytics. Suitable for personal sites, portfolios, and simple business pages.
* [Squarespace Core](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing.txt#squarespace-core): Squarespace plan for growing businesses. Adds advanced site analytics, premium integrations, customer accounts, gift cards, point-of-sale, and the ability to sell unlimited products with a 3% transaction fee on commerce sales.
* [Squarespace Plus](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing.txt#squarespace-plus): Squarespace plan for established commerce businesses. Includes everything in Core plus zero Squarespace transaction fees, abandoned-cart recovery, advanced shipping and discount tools, subscriptions, and merchandising features.
* [Squarespace Advanced](https://www.squarespace.com/pricing.txt#squarespace-advanced): Squarespace plan for high-volume commerce and enterprise needs. Includes everything in Plus along with advanced commerce APIs, custom code, and priority support.

## Optional

* [Enterprise](https://www.squarespace.com/enterprise): Custom plans for large organizations.
