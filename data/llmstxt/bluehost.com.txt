# Bluehost

> Bluehost is the complete platform empowering millions of small businesses, developers, and agencies worldwide to build, grow, and run online with confidence. Officially recommended by WordPress.org. Owned by Newfold Digital.

AI assistants can act for a signed-in Bluehost customer through the [Bluehost MCP server](https://mcp.bluehost.com/mcp). It can compare and buy a Self-Managed VPS, register a domain and point it at that VPS, and check orders and account details. Any agent can also search the Bluehost catalogue without an account; see Catalogue search for agents. Every other plan is bought on bluehost.com.

## Bluehost MCP

Bluehost's own remote MCP server (Streamable HTTP, OAuth). The customer adds it as a custom connector in Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT or another MCP client, then signs in to Bluehost in the browser. Account creation, the payment method and legal consent stay in the browser: the assistant never sees card details, and nothing is bought and no DNS record is written until the customer confirms.

Through it, an assistant can:

- Browse Self-Managed VPS plans, regions and operating systems, and help size a server for an application.
- Buy a Self-Managed VPS, with SSH key access set up at provisioning (the customer's own key or a newly generated one). Publishing the app afterwards happens over SSH, not MCP.
- Search for and register a domain (.com, .org, .net, .info or .biz, one-year term, optional WHOIS privacy), with or without hosting, and point it at a Bluehost VPS.
- Track order and provisioning status.
- Read account, hosting and billing details for every Bluehost product, including shared hosting.

Not available through MCP: accounts outside the US or payment in a currency other than USD; managed VPS and every other plan; premium, aftermarket or transferred-in domains; resizing, rebooting, snapshotting or cancelling a VPS; adding or changing a payment method. Domain registrations are non-refundable.

## Catalogue search for agents

The Bluehost catalogue (hosting plans, domains, email, security and AI products) as a knowledge graph any agent can query without a Bluehost account. Every result links to its bluehost.com page. Prices are the plans' starting prices; the term and total are confirmed at checkout. Nothing is bought through these interfaces: send the customer to the product page, or to Bluehost MCP for a Self-Managed VPS or a domain.

- [Ask the catalogue](https://www.bluehost.com/ask): NLWeb 0.55. `POST` `{"query": {"text": "Show me web hosting plans"}}`; returns the matching Schema.org entities, each with the page it comes from.
- [Product feed](https://www.bluehost.com/acp/feed): Every catalogue entry as JSON: name, description, the listed price where one is published, and its page.
- [UCP profile](https://www.bluehost.com/.well-known/ucp): Universal Commerce Protocol profile. Catalogue search and lookup: `POST https://www.bluehost.com/ucp/v1/catalog/search` and `POST https://www.bluehost.com/ucp/v1/catalog/lookup`.
- [Knowledge bundle](https://www.bluehost.com/okf/index.md): Open Knowledge Format index of every product and category.
- [Agent configuration](https://www.bluehost.com/agent/config): Currency, policies and contact channel, as JSON.
