# GumGum

> GumGum is The Mindset Company™ — a global advertising technology company that identifies the moments consumers are most receptive to brand messages, and delivers mindset-driven campaigns across CTV, contextual, and high-impact display at scale.

Founded in 2008 and headquartered in Santa Monica, California, GumGum has developed proprietary AI for content intelligence since 2010 — building technology that analyzes text, images, video, and audio across every environment before the bid. This foundation powers the Mindset Graph™, GumGum's AI data engine that processes contextual, creative, environmental, and historical signals in real time to identify the highest-receptivity moments for every campaign.

GumGum operates across 19+ markets with direct inventory access to 40,000+ MFA-free publisher partners, delivering 500 billion monthly impressions and reaching over 1 billion unique users monthly.

## What We Do

- [Contextual Advertising](https://gumgum.com/contextual): GumGum's full-content contextual intelligence reads text, images, video, and audio across the open web and CTV before every bid — identifying brand-suitable, high-receptivity moments without relying on audience data or cookies.
- [CTV & Video Advertising](https://gumgum.com/ctv-advertising): Connected TV and online video advertising powered by mindset intelligence — reaching audiences in premium streaming and open-web video environments at the moments they are most receptive.
- [Omnichannel Advertising](https://gumgum.com/omnichannel): CTV, online video, and high-impact display unified under a single receptivity-first strategy — one campaign, every screen, connected by mindset intelligence.

## Our Technology

- [The Mindset Graph™](https://gumgum.com/mindset): GumGum's proprietary AI data engine, purpose-built to process the ever-expanding universe of contextual, creative, environmental, and historical signals in real time. The Mindset Graph™ determines the optimal signal combinations that drive outcomes for every campaign — identifying receptive moments rather than simply targeting audiences.
