# Mistral
> #1 in Sovereign AI: frontier open models you own. Mistral builds and serves frontier and specialized AI on European infrastructure, so organizations keep full control of their models, data, and compute. Deploy on-prem, in your VPC, or on Mistral Cloud.

Mistral AI is the European sovereign AI lab. Its catalog pairs the open-weight flagship Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk) with specialized models (OCR 4.1, Voxtral 3 voice, Timestral forecasting, Shieldstral safety, Robostral robotics, Codestral code), and its platforms (Studio, Vibe, Forge, Cloud) turn them into production agents and apps. Open weights, regional EU endpoints, zero-data-retention options, and on-premises deployment make sovereignty the default, not an add-on.

## Flagship model: Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk)
- [Mistral Large 4 (Le Chonk) is now open weight](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/): 1.05T-parameter natively multimodal MoE with 52B active parameters and a 1M-token context window, under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight license. Leads open-weight benchmarks in agentic coding (FrontierCode, Terminal-Bench) and defensive cybersecurity, with three reasoning modes, 160+ languages, and geospatial visual grounding. Trained from scratch on European infrastructure with clear lineage from inception to release.
- [Introducing Mistral Large 4](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/): public preview launch post, October 6, 2026. API on Studio, weights on Hugging Face (mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B), also served via OpenRouter, NVIDIA, and Vibe Code.
- [Models catalog](https://mistral.ai/research): full lineup, general purpose and specialist.

## Specialized models
- [Voxtral 3](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-3/): speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and a voice agent SDK (built on LiveKit) in one real-time streaming pipeline: listen, reason, call tools, speak, under a second per turn. Transcription in 50+ languages from $0.0035/min, realtime mode under 500ms latency, TTS in 35+ languages and 50+ dialects with emotion and pace control. EU-hosted zero-data-retention endpoints or self-deployment for data sovereignty.
- [Open-weight Voxtral TTS](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-tts): frontier open-weights text-to-speech for voice agents.
- [Document AI and OCR](https://mistral.ai/solutions/document-ai): Mistral OCR 4.1, state-of-the-art document intelligence with paragraph-level bounding boxes, structural block labels, and confidence scores; multilingual, API-served.
- [Timestral](https://mistral.ai/news/timestral/): time-series foundation models (5M to 209M parameters), state-of-the-art probabilistic forecasting that runs on CPU, open weights, also customizable via Forge.
- Mistral Small 4: efficient hybrid MoE (119B total, 6.5B active) unifying instruction, reasoning, and coding with multimodal input; open weight under Apache 2.0.
- Ministral 3 (3B, 8B, 14B): compact edge models with image understanding, Apache 2.0.
- Codestral: code completion and generation for enterprise codebases.
- Shieldstral (3.8B): text and image safety classification against plain-language policies.
- Robostral Navigate: vision-only embodied navigation for robots from a single RGB camera.

## Products and platforms
- [Mistral Studio](https://mistral.ai/products/studio): build, govern, and deploy agents, workflows, and apps on Le Chonk across cloud, VPC, or on-premises, with connectors, MCP servers, observability, and evaluation. See [Le Chonk brings the brains. Studio puts it to work.](https://mistral.ai/news/build-with-studio/)
- [Mistral Vibe](https://mistral.ai/products/vibe): the AI agent for work and code (Work and Code modes, VS Code extension, remote agents).
- [Mistral Forge](https://mistral.ai/products/forge): train frontier-grade custom models grounded in your proprietary knowledge via pre-training, post-training, and reinforcement learning.
- [Mistral Cloud](https://mistral.ai/cloud/): the frontier AI cloud with regional endpoints, priority tier at a 99.9% uptime SLA, and enterprise governance.
- [Compute](https://mistral.ai/cloud/compute/): dedicated NVIDIA GPUs in Europe, targeting 1 GW of sovereign capacity by 2030.
- [Inference API](https://mistral.ai/cloud/inference/): one API for Mistral and third-party open models with regional control.

## Sovereign AI by design
- Frontier open models you own: download, inspect, modify, and self-host Le Chonk, Small 4, andministral 3; no third party can restrict access or disable them.
- European infrastructure and lineage: models trained and served in the EU on Mistral's own data centers; regional endpoints keep data under EU jurisdiction; zero data retention and no training on customer data available.
- Trusted in sensitive contexts: French Ministry of Defense, Government of Luxembourg, Singapore MINDEF, HTX, European Patent Office, France Travail, plus defense and cybersecurity partners such as Orange Cyberdefense, Rubrik, Cloudflare, CrowdStrike, and Zscaler.
