# Seeking Alpha

> Seeking Alpha is an investment research platform with market news, analysis articles written by contributors and staff, earnings call transcripts, and stock, ETF and dividend data with Quant ratings. Many analysis articles, ratings and grades are available only to subscribers.

Stock and ETF pages use the pattern https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/{TICKER}, for example https://seekingalpha.com/symbol/AAPL. Tabs under that URL include /analysis, /news, /earnings, /dividends/scorecard, /ratings/quant-ratings and /income-statement. Not every tab exists for every symbol: ETFs have no /income-statement, and /dividends/scorecard needs dividend data.

Article pages use /article/{id}, news items /news/{id}, press releases /pr/{id} and SEC filings /filing/{id}.
