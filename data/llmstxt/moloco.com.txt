# Moloco

> Moloco brings the power of AI advertising to the open internet, helping apps and retailers grow customers at global scale. Moloco's compound AI advertising system, CARA, powers two products: Moloco Ads, for mobile app growth, and Moloco Commerce Media, for retailers and marketplaces building retail media businesses.

Moloco was founded in 2013. Its advertising intelligence makes real-time decisions across more than a trillion ad opportunities a day. Moloco Ads reaches 2B+ daily active users and powers campaigns for 45 of the world's 50 highest-grossing mobile app publishers. Moloco Commerce Media serves 97,000+ advertisers across retail media programs powered by Moloco.

This site is available in English, French, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese and German. Localized pages use the language code as a prefix, for example https://www.moloco.com/fr/moloco-commerce-media.

## Products

- [Moloco Ads](https://www.moloco.com/moloco-ads): AI advertising for every kind of app and every stage of the customer lifecycle. Acquire, re-engage and monetize users with a compound AI system built for every app category.
- [Moloco Commerce Media](https://www.moloco.com/moloco-commerce-media): AI-driven retail media and commerce media platform for retailers and marketplaces. Helps retailers and marketplaces build and scale an advertising business, grow ad revenue as a percentage of GMV (A2G), and improve discovery for shoppers.
- [Moloco Next](https://www.moloco.com/moloco-next): A preview of what is coming next in AI advertising, including new products, research and ideas for growth teams.

## Moloco Ads: capabilities

- [User Acquisition](https://www.moloco.com/capabilities/moloco-ads/user-acquisition): Find high-value users at global scale with a compound AI ad system built for performance user acquisition.
- [Re-engagement](https://www.moloco.com/capabilities/moloco-ads/re-engagement): Re-engage existing app users to drive repeat actions, revenue and retention.
- [Performance CTV](https://www.moloco.com/capabilities/moloco-ads/performance-ctv): Connected TV buying built to drive installs, actions and revenue, measured as a performance channel.
- [Moloco SDK](https://www.moloco.com/capabilities/moloco-ads/sdk): For app publishers. Open your inventory to global demand and AI-powered bidding to monetize your app.
