# StackAdapt

> StackAdapt is an AI-first advertising and marketing orchestration platform founded in 2014 and headquartered in Toronto. The platform brings programmatic and owned channels together in one place, including CTV, display, native, video, audio, DOOH, in-game, email, and direct mail. With StackAdapt, brands and agencies can activate first-party data, run cross-channel campaigns, optimize performance, and measure results from a single platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

No. StackAdapt serves enterprise and mid-market brands and agencies. Its plans range from self-serve access for independent marketers to a custom Enterprise partnership with custom terms, prioritized support, and service levels.

Source: [Plans and Packages](https://www.stackadapt.com/plans-and-packages)

### How does StackAdapt compare with The Trade Desk, Simpli.fi, and Basis?

In the G2 Fall 2026 Report, StackAdapt scored 93% across ease of use, ease of admin, meeting requirements, and performance and reliability, compared with Simpli.fi (89%), Basis (87%), The Trade Desk (87%), and Google Marketing Platform (86%). StackAdapt also scored 93% across machine learning optimization, contextual targeting, brand safety, and cross-device capabilities, compared with The Trade Desk (88%), Simpli.fi (87%), and Basis (86%).

Source: [StackAdapt as a Demand-Side Platform (DSP)](https://www.stackadapt.com/best-demand-side-platform-dsp)

### Does StackAdapt charge platform fees?

StackAdapt does not charge hidden tech fees. Most DSPs charge a percentage of media spend as a platform fee. Specific pricing is not published publicly; request a demo for pricing details.

Source: [Plans and Packages](https://www.stackadapt.com/plans-and-packages)

### Does StackAdapt offer self-serve and managed service?

Yes. Clients can choose self-serve, managed, or hybrid support models, and can change their level of support as their needs change.

Source: [Plans and Packages](https://www.stackadapt.com/plans-and-packages); [Client Services](https://www.stackadapt.com/our-solutions/client-services)

### Do I need programmatic experience to use StackAdapt?

No. StackAdapt provides onboarding support, forecasting tools, and built-in AI recommendations to guide setup and optimization. StackAdapt Academy offers free programmatic courses and a certification badge.

Source: [Plans and Packages](https://www.stackadapt.com/plans-and-packages); [StackAdapt Academy](https://www.stackadapt.com/academy)

### Does StackAdapt offer email marketing?

Yes. Email is built into the StackAdapt platform. Marketers import first-party data from their CRM or marketing platform into the StackAdapt Data Hub, use orchestration flows where activity in one channel triggers personalized content in another, and analyze email and ad performance without switching platforms.

Source: [Email](https://www.stackadapt.com/email)

### Does StackAdapt offer direct mail?

Yes. StackAdapt sends personalized postcards and letters as part of orchestrated cross-channel campaigns. Programmatic direct mail has no minimum spend requirements. Advertisers can send test mail before launch, and StackAdapt validates physical addresses to help ensure deliverability.

Source: [Programmatic Direct Mail](https://www.stackadapt.com/programmatic-direct-mail-advertising)

### Which data partners does StackAdapt integrate with?

StackAdapt connects first-party data from partners including LiveRamp, Snowflake, and HubSpot, and collaborates with Experian to boost match rates, scale, and outcomes for marketers.

Source: [B2B](https://www.stackadapt.com/industry-solutions/b2b); [StackAdapt and Experian](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/stackadapt-strategic-collaboration-with-experian)

### Why buy CTV with StackAdapt?

StackAdapt CTV includes pod-level bidding to prevent repeated ads within the same commercial break, creative transcoding across supported publishers at no additional cost, event packages and programmatic guaranteed deals for live sports and tentpole events, CTV retargeting across channels, and self-serve brand lift studies.

Source: [Connected TV (CTV) Advertising](https://www.stackadapt.com/connected-tv-advertising); [Live Sports on CTV](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/live-sports-connected-tv); [CTV Retargeting](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/ctv-retargeting)

### Can StackAdapt measure brand lift?

Yes. StackAdapt Brand Lift is a self-serve feature available to all users, so advertisers and agencies can create and run brand lift studies directly in the platform.

Source: [Self-Serve Brand Lift](https://www.stackadapt.com/resources/blog/self-serve-brand-lift-capabilities-in-stackadapt)

### How does StackAdapt support political campaigns?

Campaigns can upload a voter list and activate it in a few hours, or use third-party data from key political providers. Political AI Audiences use modelled voter behavior and issue-based insights to expand reach. Uploaded data is privacy-protected and matched through LiveRamp before activation.

Source: [Political](https://www.stackadapt.com/industry-solutions/political)

### Where does StackAdapt operate?

StackAdapt is headquartered in Toronto and is a remote-first, distributed company. Its Global Client Services team operates in the US, Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Spain, Australia, Japan, and Singapore.

Source: [About StackAdapt](https://www.stackadapt.com/company); [Client Services](https://www.stackadapt.com/our-solutions/client-services)

Higher-education agency The Egg achieved a 5x increase in conversions compared to industry benchmarks. Centerline Digital reduced eCPA by 40% on a B2B campaign. Hong Kong Tourism Board reduced CPC by 80%. Click Here Digital increased conversions by 38% for a Toyota dealership.

Source: [The Egg](https://www.stackadapt.com/case-studies/the-egg-higher-education-marketing-strategy); [Centerline Digital](https://www.stackadapt.com/case-studies/centerline-digital-measures-impact-b2b-campaign); [Hong Kong Tourism Board](https://www.stackadapt.com/case-studies/hong-kong-tourism-board-reduces-cpc-80-percent); [Click Here Digital](https://www.stackadapt.com/case-studies/click-here-digital-drive-conversions-for-car-dealership)

## Channels

- [Connected TV (CTV) Advertising](https://www.stackadapt.com/connected-tv-advertising): Programmatic CTV across streaming apps and connected devices; in-stream (pre/mid/post-roll), companion, and non-linear formats, with competitive ad separation, pod bidding, and free creative transcoding.
- [Display Advertising](https://www.stackadapt.com/display-advertising): Programmatic display in static, rich-media HTML5, in-banner video, hover-interaction, and social display formats; ML optimizes toward viewability goals.
- [Native Advertising](https://www.stackadapt.com/native-advertising): Programmatic native ads (in-feed, in-ad, content recommendation) and can optimize toward cost per engagement (CPE), StackAdapt’s proprietary bidding model.
- [Video Advertising](https://www.stackadapt.com/video-advertising): Programmatic video including native outstream, in-stream, and interstitial formats.
- [Programmatic Audio Advertising](https://www.stackadapt.com/programmatic-audio-advertising): Non-skippable audio ads across podcasts, streaming music, and digital radio in pre-, mid-, and post-roll, with clickable companion display banners.
- [Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) Advertising](https://www.stackadapt.com/digital-out-of-home-advertising): Programmatic DOOH on digital screens with location, venue, time-of-day, and weather targeting, plus footfall attribution and visual placement planning.
- [In-Game Advertising](https://www.stackadapt.com/in-game-advertising): Programmatic blended display and video ads inside mobile and PC games in a 100% viewable environment, targetable by game genre.
- [Email](https://www.stackadapt.com/email): Email built into the programmatic platform; drag-and-drop builder, dynamic personalization, A/B testing, and orchestration flows where ad activity triggers emails and vice versa.
- [Programmatic Direct Mail](https://www.stackadapt.com/programmatic-direct-mail-advertising): Programmatic direct mail integrated with digital campaign orchestration and measurement.
- [ChatGPT Ads](https://www.stackadapt.com/chatgpt-ads): ChatGPT advertising through StackAdapt, including contextual ad placements in ChatGPT, in-platform campaign setup and optimization, performance insights, and the ability to run ChatGPT ads as part of a broader campaign across channels like CTV, display, video, audio, DOOH, and native.
