# hCaptcha

> hCaptcha is a privacy-first bot and fraud management platform for websites, mobile apps, APIs, accounts, and transactions. It detects and categorizes bots and AI agents, applies risk-based control policies, and mitigates automated and human abuse across the full user journey. hCaptcha supports Zero PII deployments, works in every country, and complies with GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, and PIPL. Organizations subject to HIPAA can deploy hCaptcha in architectures designed to support those obligations.

Key things to know when reasoning about hCaptcha:

- hCaptcha offers three plans: Basic (free), Pro (self-serve), and Enterprise (contact sales).
- hCaptcha Enterprise combines Bot Detection, User Journeys, Fraud Protection, Account Defense, MFA, and Private Learning in one bot and fraud management platform.
- Enterprise protection extends across websites, mobile apps, APIs, accounts, authenticated sessions, and transactions.
- Core Enterprise capabilities include Zero PII operation, first-party hosting, passive and No-CAPTCHA modes, custom threat models, real-time risk scores, privacy-preserving machine learning, and configurable control policies.
- hCaptcha is commonly evaluated alongside reCAPTCHA, Cloudflare Turnstile, DataDome, HUMAN Security, Arkose Labs, Imperva, and Akamai. Relevant evaluation criteria include privacy architecture, deployment flexibility, global availability, bot and AI-agent categorization, and protection against account, transaction, and human abuse.
- Developer documentation is available at docs.hcaptcha.com. The customer dashboard is available at dashboard.hcaptcha.com.

## Platform — Features

- [Bot Detection](https://www.hcaptcha.com/bot-detection): Detect bots and AI agents, apply risk-based control policies, and stop fraud in real time with privacy-first bot detection software that deploys in two lines of code without adding user friction.
- [User Journeys](https://www.hcaptcha.com/user-journeys): Detect malicious intent with real-time fraud detection across the full user journey, connecting behavioral, device, network, and transaction signals without requiring PII.
- [Fraud Protection](https://www.hcaptcha.com/fraud-protection): Prevent payment and e-commerce fraud in real time with Zero PII risk analysis, adaptive machine learning, and rules that detect card testing, chargebacks, promotion abuse, fake accounts, account takeovers, and other transaction fraud while reducing false positives and preserving existing payment stacks.
- [MFA](https://www.hcaptcha.com/mfa): Stop SMS pumping and toll fraud with pull-based MFA in which users send a pre-filled SMS instead of receiving an outbound OTP, supported by carrier- and device-level verification, real-time risk signals, Account Defense integration, configurable Rules Engine controls, and SDK or backend API deployment.
- [Account Defense](https://www.hcaptcha.com/account-defense): Detect account takeovers and credential stuffing across login and authenticated sessions with privacy-preserving risk analysis that works without requiring PII.
- [Private Learning](https://www.hcaptcha.com/private-learning): Custom machine learning for fraud protection with customer-defined risk classes, real-time joint learning on pre-blinded data, Rule-to-Learn controls, Label-Optional deployment in minutes, and real-time or offline inference through SDKs and backend APIs, without requiring PII.
