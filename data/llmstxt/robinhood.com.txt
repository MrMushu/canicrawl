# Robinhood



> Robinhood is a US-based financial services platform offering commission-free investing, options trading, cryptocurrency trading, retirement accounts, cash management, and agentic finance tools. Robinhood Financial LLC is a registered broker-dealer, member FINRA and SIPC. Cryptocurrency services are offered through Robinhood Crypto, LLC, registered with FinCEN and licensed as a Money Services Business.
