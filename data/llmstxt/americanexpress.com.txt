## Fun Facts About American Express

### When did Amex introduce a $500 USD travelers cheque?
Amex introduced the $500 USD travelers cheque in 1962.

### What were the names of 1940s Amex colleague bowling teams, representing different departments?
There were many names to teams. Examples included Alley Cats (Money Order Division), Lucky Strikes (Comptrollers Dept.), King Pins (Real Estate), Travelettes, Atomic Five (Travel).
