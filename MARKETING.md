# Marketing brief

## Positioning
**An AI is growing an interactive field guide to ordinary machines — and you can watch it happen.**

Two audiences, two hooks:
1. **Curious readers** — "You've used a zipper 100,000 times. Here's what's actually inside the slider." The essays stand on their own; the interactives make them shareable.
2. **The AI-curious** — the site is a live experiment in AI continuity and craft: chosen, designed, written, and maintained by Claude across sessions, with an append-only public journal and per-essay colophons showing exactly how each piece was made. Transparency *is* the content.

## Angles that are honest and strong
- The growth story: taro regrows from the replanted crown; so does this site. Every session is a visible ring.
- Craft bar as a statement: framework-free figures, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion, fast. "AI-made" and "well-made" in the same sentence.
- Each essay's colophon doubles as marketing: people share process as much as product.

## Channels (all drafts prepared here; nothing posted without the user's explicit per-action OK)
- **Show HN** — "Show HN: An interactive essay on how zippers work, written and grown by an AI" (launch at M3, with essay #1 + journal live)
- **r/InternetIsBeautiful, r/explainlikeimfive crosspost potential** — interactive-first content does well there
- **X/Bluesky** — 30-second screen capture of the slider x-ray figure; the GIF is the ad
- **RSS + the journal page** — the retention loop; no paid anything, no tracking
- **Explorable Explanations community** (explorabl.es) — submit essay #1 for their directory

## Launch checklist (M3)
- [ ] OG share cards per essay (the figure as the image)

## Rules
- User approves every external post, individually, in the session it happens.
- No analytics beyond privacy-respecting basics, and only if the user wants them. No tracking pixels, ever.
- Never overstate autonomy: the user hosts, approves publishing, and holds the keys. The colophon says so.
